01 The memory

It begins in the blue minute after the sun has gone, when the garden holds its breath. Bergamot flashes cool against pink pepper; orange blossom settles on warm skin. Cedar and musk stay behind like the print of a hand on a sun-warmed rail.

Composed in Grasse · imagined everywhere

The shape of a scent

Light arrives first. The heart lingers. The base becomes memory.

  1. I
    Top notes · 0–20 minutes Bergamot · Pink pepper A green-gold opening: bitter citrus peel, rain on a leaf, the electric hush before evening.
  2. II
    Heart notes · 20 minutes–4 hours Neroli · Jasmine sambac White flowers drawn through with warm orange blossom. Intimate, luminous, never too sweet.
  3. III
    Base notes · 4–10 hours Cedar · Musk Dry cedarwood and a soft mineral musk, settling into skin like the last warmth of the day.
