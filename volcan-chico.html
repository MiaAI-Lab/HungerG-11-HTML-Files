Our story / San Lázaro del Humo

Born in a
bus garage.

Two cousins. One very loud idea. Cero corporate money.

In the invented, Oaxaca-inspired town of San Lázaro del Humo, cousins Itzel and Beto took over a converted bus garage and started fermenting hot sauce in repurposed food-grade barrels.

Ninety days later, the neighborhood had three new favorites and a suspiciously pleasant smell. We still make every batch there: hand-chopped peppers, sun-loving ferments, labels stuck on slightly crooked.

We make hot sauce for people who say “just a little” and then ask for more. ¡Salud, cabrones!
