TITLE
Luminote — Piano with Falling Lights

PROMPT
Build a single self-contained instrument called Luminote, "Piano with falling lights". The stage is dark (#0a0a12, #101019, #161620) with warm key light #e8c59a and #f6dbb7 and pale type #f4f0e7. The sentence on the page: "An instrument made of light."

The keyboard is three octaves, C3 to C6, drawn as real white and black keys. Notes can sound together. Play with the mouse, with several fingers on a touch screen, or with the computer keyboard: black keys on the Q–P row, white keys on the A–L row and the row below, as a small help dialog explains. Every note releases a falling light on a visualizer above the keys. A visible sound control can mute the synthesized piano. Sound is produced in the browser with the Web Audio API.

Three modes: Free play ("Your stage, your light. Play anything."); Listen, which performs a chosen melody — Ode to Joy, Für Elise, Twinkle Twinkle Little Star, Gymnopédie No. 1 — at a tempo the visitor sets, with restart and pause; Learn, which lights the next key and waits for the visitor to play it. A control shows or hides note names.

The first view should already be a piano, not a loading state. Reduced motion keeps the keys and the modes and calms the falling lights. No external fonts, samples, or libraries.

DESCRIPTION
A three-octave piano made of light, from C3 to C6, on a dark stage. Every note leaves a falling glow. Free play is the open keyboard. Listen performs Ode to Joy, Für Elise, Twinkle Twinkle Little Star or Gymnopédie No. 1 at a chosen tempo. Learn lights the next key and waits. The keys answer the mouse, several fingers at once, and two rows of the computer keyboard.
